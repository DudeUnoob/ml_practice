Question: What is a token in language modeling?
Answer: A discrete unit of text, such as a character, subword, or word piece.

Question: What is the purpose of a causal mask in GPT?
Answer: It prevents the model from attending to future tokens while predicting the next token.

Question: What is self-attention?
Answer: A mechanism where each token builds a weighted combination of other tokens in the sequence.

Question: What is the difference between training and inference?
Answer: Training updates model weights using data; inference uses fixed weights to generate outputs.

Question: What is a checkpoint?
Answer: A saved snapshot of model weights and configuration during or after training.

Question: What is overfitting?
Answer: When a model memorizes training data and performs poorly on unseen examples.

Question: What is a learning rate?
Answer: The step size used when updating model parameters during optimization.

Question: What is weight decay?
Answer: A regularization technique that penalizes large parameter values.
